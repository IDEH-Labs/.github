## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you identify a security or privacy vulnerability in any of our software:

1. **Do not open a public issue.** Please avoid disclosing potential vulnerabilities publicly until we have had an opportunity to review and mitigate them.
2. **Private Reporting Options:**
   - **GitHub Security Advisories:** Use the "Report a vulnerability" button in the **Security** tab of the respective repository.
   - **Email:** Send details to our security contact at `security@ideh.top` or `jpi59@icloud.com`.
3. **What to include:**
   - Clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
   - Steps to reproduce or proof-of-concept (PoC).
   - Affected version(s) and Android API levels tested.

---

## Our Commitment

- **Acknowledgment:** We will acknowledge receipt of your vulnerability report within **48 hours**.
- **Assessment & Fix:** We will evaluate the report, verify the issue, and prepare an update or fix.
- **Credit:** We are glad to credit researchers in our release notes and changelogs if desired.
